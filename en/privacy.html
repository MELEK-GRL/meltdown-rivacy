In short: Melty Slice has no accounts and no sign-in. As the developer, we do not collect your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

1. Controller

This policy applies to the mobile game Melty Slice: Pizza Run (the "Game"), developed and published by MELEK-GRL (the "Developer", "we"). It is written with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") in mind.

2. What we collect and what we don't

InformationStatus
Name, email, phone, address, IDNot collected. There are no accounts, sign-in or registration.
Location, camera, microphone, contacts, photosNot accessed. The Game does not request these permissions.
Game progress and settings (best distance, unlocked pizzas, missions, language, sound and quality preferences)Stored locally on your device only. Never sent to the Developer.

3. Internet connection

The Game requires an internet connection. To check whether you are online, it sends a short, empty request to standard connectivity-check addresses (clients3.google.com and captive.apple.com). The request contains no game data about you. As with any internet request, those servers may process your IP address and basic technical information under their own privacy policies: Google, Apple.

4. Device backups

Android and iOS may back up app data, including your game progress, through their own backup services (Google account backup, iCloud). These backups are governed by your settings and by Google's and Apple's policies. The Developer cannot access this data.

5. Third-party components

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics and advertising services are disabled in this version. The Game is downloaded through app stores (Google Play, App Store), whose download and payment processes are subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising (future)

This version of the Game shows no ads. If ads are added later, the ad provider may process data such as the advertising ID. Before any such change, this policy will be updated, and your consent will be requested where the law requires it (e.g. GDPR/KVKK consent, App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

7. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. We recommend that users under 18 use the Game with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 and the GDPR, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information, rectification and erasure, to object, and to lodge a complaint. Because the Developer stores no personal data about you, you can delete all game data on your device at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing its data in your device settings. You can send requests through the contact link below. You also keep your right to complain to the competent data protection authority.

9. Security

Game data stays on your device and is not transferred to the Developer's servers. Keeping your device secure (screen lock, up-to-date OS) helps protect it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published at this address with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes, such as adding ads, will be announced in the Game or on its store page.
